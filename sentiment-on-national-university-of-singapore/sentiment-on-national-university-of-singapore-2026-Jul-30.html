1. Singapore is the only Southeast Asian nation with two universities in the world's AI top 10

NTU held its No. 2 global spot and NUS climbed four places to 5th in the newly released 2026–2027 U.S. News Best Global Universities subject ranking for artificial intelligence, the only Southeast Asian country to place two institutions in the world top 10. The ranking, released in June and covering 2,250 universities that each published at least 200 AI papers between 2020 and 2024, was otherwise swept by mainland China, which took 8 of the top 10 spots; no U.S. or European university made the cut. NUS also placed 1st globally in optics, 5th in materials science and 6th in computer science in the same subject-ranking cycle, extending the string of strong subject-ranking stories NUS has generated this month. The result reinforces NUS's positioning as a genuine AI-research heavyweight rather than just a strong regional player.

Sources: VnExpress International, U.S. News Best Global Universities — NUS profile

3. NUS Business School designs a second leadership programme for Vietnam's GELEX Group
