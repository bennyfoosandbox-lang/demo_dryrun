2. NUS ties 32nd globally and leads Southeast Asia in the new U.S. News mathematics rankings, with top-10 finishes in eight subjects

The freshly released 2026–2027 U.S. News Best Global Universities subject rankings place NUS 32nd in the world for mathematics, tied with Imperial College London and the highest of any Southeast Asian institution — NTU was the only other Singapore school to make the list, at 234th. 5. NUS engineers wire up Kent Ridge as a live flood laboratory to crack Singapore's rainfall-to-runoff puzzle

Professor Simone Fatichi of the NUS College of Design and Engineering's Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering has spent 22 months instrumenting an 8.6-hectare stretch of campus — from the Central Library to NUS Business School — with meteorological stations, soil-moisture sensors, groundwater wells and drain-flow sensors logging data every five minutes, in what NUS describes as the first study of its kind tracking rainfall-to-runoff behaviour in a tropical city. The team found that soils which normally absorb water quickly can become nearly saturated during intense storms, producing flash-flood-like runoff almost as fast as paved surfaces — a risk the researchers hope to model city-wide to help authorities forecast flood hotspots and warn the public in advance. Published as the second instalment of an NUS sustainability-research series (following a companion piece on coastal protection), the story continues a steady cadence of climate-resilience research positioning NUS as a applied-science contributor to Singapore's adaptation planning.

Sources: NUS News
